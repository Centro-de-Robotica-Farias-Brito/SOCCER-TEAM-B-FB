# FB Futebol Clube — Team B

## RoboCupJunior Soccer Vision 2026

We are a student robotics team from Ceará, Brazil, developing autonomous soccer robots that integrate embedded electronics, mechanical design, computer vision, and predictive motion planning.

## Our Robots

Our team has developed two robots with complementary roles: an offensive player and a defensive player.

## Technical Innovations

### 1. Efficient Computer Vision
A pixel-level edge detection algorithm based on local chromatic analysis, integer arithmetic, lookup tables, and circular buffers.

### 2. Hierarchical Eisenstein Spatial Encoding
A compact, 24-bit hierarchical spatial representation integrated with predictive autonomous motion planning.

## Documentation
Technical diagrams, source code, hardware designs, testing procedures, and experimental results will be published in this repository.

## Development Status
Computational tests have been conducted for the Eisenstein-based representation. Embedded and physical interception validation are still pending.
